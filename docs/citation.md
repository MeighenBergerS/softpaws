# Citation

If softpaws is useful in your work, please cite the method paper.

```txt
@article{MeighenBerger:softpaws,
  author  = {Meighen-Berger, Stephan A.},
  title   = {{Analytical High-Energy Muon Transport for Neutrino Telescopes}},
  year    = {2026},
}
```

The entry is updated with the arXiv number and the journal reference when
they exist.

## Citing what softpaws is built on

The results depend on several inputs that carry their own references. Cite
the ones your analysis uses.

**The earlier analytic treatment.** softpaws keeps the drift-diffusion
expansion of the same collision operator as a limit and a cross-check of the
transport exponent. If you use that path, cite Palmisano, Redigolo, Tammaro
and Tesi, *The soft volume of ultra-high energy neutrinos experiments*,
[arXiv:2607.13143](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13143), and its companion,
*Exploring ultra-high energy neutrino experiments through the lens of the
transport equation*, JHEP 03 (2026) 223,
[arXiv:2507.10665](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10665).

**The loss coefficients.** PROPOSAL supplies the bremsstrahlung, pair
production and photonuclear parametrizations the kernel is calibrated to:
Koehne et al., Comput. Phys. Commun. 184 (2013) 2070, and Dunsch et al.,
Comput. Phys. Commun. 242 (2019) 132.

**The cross section.** Bertone, Gauld and Rojo, BGR18,
[arXiv:1808.02034](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.02034).

**The Earth model.** Dziewonski and Anderson, PREM, Phys. Earth Planet.
Inter. 25 (1981) 297.

**The IceCube data.** The IceTracks-DR2 release, DOI
[10.7910/DVN/MMIIZA](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/MMIIZA), with its paper
[arXiv:2605.19040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19040).

**The atmospheric background.** MCEq: Fedynitch et al., EPJ Web Conf. 99
(2015) 08001.

**The other detectors' effective areas.** KM3NeT/ARCA: Eur. Phys. J. C 84
(2024) 885. P-ONE: the ICRC 2023 proceedings. TRIDENT: Nature Astronomy 7
(2023) 1497, and the 2025 simulation study.
