softpaws

softpaws — a first-principles forward model for UHE neutrino telescopes.

Implements the analytic muon transport of Meighen-Berger, Estimating High-Energy Neutrino Effective Areas from Muon Propagation (2026). The locally scale-invariant collision operator is diagonalized by power laws, so a muon's whole loss history collapses to a single transport exponent Phi(s), which then serves as a first-passage generator for the range of the muon and for the detector response built on it. The result is benchmarked against the published effective areas of IceCube, KM3NeT/ARCA, P-ONE and TRIDENT, against IceCube's own instrument response functions (effective area + smearing matrix), and against IceCube data.

Modules:

  • constants –

    Units (the code computes in GeV, cm, s and rad), physical constants and defaults.

  • data –

    Load the IceCube releases and the published curves of the other detectors.

  • detectors –

    Published geometry, medium, and optics for each site.

  • fluxes –

    Power laws, the published IceCube fits, and the atmospheric background.

  • transport –

    The loss kernel, the transport exponent, ranges, Earth, and the tau channel.

  • response –

    Forward models mapping a neutrino flux to an effective area or event rate.

  • comparison –

    Likelihoods, the event benchmark, and single-track energy reconstruction.
